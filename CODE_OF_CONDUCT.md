# Code of Conduct

IMRA-1 is committed to a collaborative, harassment-free research environment. All
participants agree to:

1. **Respect** – treat every contributor with dignity, regardless of background, expertise,
   or affiliation.
   
2. **Inclusivity** – encourage diverse perspectives and constructive critique focused on
   ideas, not individuals.
3. **Integrity** – credit sources, share data responsibly, and avoid misrepresentation of
   experimental results.
4. **Safety** – report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers at `ntokozo.dlamini.xyz@gmail.com`.

Unacceptable behaviors include harassment, discrimination, trolling, doxxing, or other
conduct that compromises research integrity or personal safety.

Maintainers may warn, remove, or ban contributors who violate the Code of Conduct.
